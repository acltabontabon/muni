# The Muni name and logo

The source code is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. That license does not grant rights to
the project's name or logo (section 6 of the license). This note is separate from the software
license and doesn't change it.

- **You can** refer to Muni by name, say that your work is based on or compatible with Muni, and
  link to the project.
- **Please don't** present a modified version, a fork, or a hosted service of your own as "Muni",
  or use the Muni mark (the lowercase *m* with a dot and its reflection) or the munimuni.app domain
  in a way that suggests it is the original project or endorsed by it. Give your version its own
  name and logo.

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
