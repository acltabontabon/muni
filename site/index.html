How Muni works

  1. 01 During the sprint

    Set it down while it’s fresh.

    A thought takes ten seconds to write on Tuesday and is gone by Friday. Muni keeps a quiet place open all sprint — on your phone, between tasks. If the signal drops, it waits and sends when you’re back, and tells you plainly when a thought has reached the sprint.

  2. 02 Sealed

    Your team can’t read them yet.

    Not the facilitator, not your manager. Until collection closes, the only thoughts you can see are your own — and you can still change them.

  3. 03 Opening the sprint

    One batch. Random order. No names.

    Everything is revealed at once, shuffled. Shared screens never show who wrote a thought, or anything that ties one person’s thoughts together.

  4. 04 Before the retro

    Thoughts become themes.

    If it helps, the facilitator gathers them into themes by hand, each with a question to open it. The original words are never replaced.

05 The retro

One topic. The actual words.

Four steps, each one question: did last time’s experiments help, what matters most, the talk, and what to try. Everyone votes privately on their phone; then the shared screen takes the topics in that order, with what people wrote at the centre — not a summary of it.

  • A way in for the quiet

    When it helps, the facilitator asks how a topic showed up. One tap answers; a line is optional; nothing is shown until it’s shared, and never with a name. Nobody is called on.

  • A companion on every phone

    Follow the stage, vote privately, answer a check-in in one tap, add to the discussion without your name, or say “I’ll own this” to an experiment.

  • Disagreement stays visible

    Opposing views sit side by side in their own words. Nothing is averaged into a paragraph.

One to three concrete changes, each with an owner who says yes, a sign that it helped, and a date to revisit.

Privacy

Your reflections are for your team.

Your reflections are for your team.

Muni uses your account information and contributions to provide and protect the service. We don’t sell your personal data, use your contributions for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

Sealed, then shared without names

Until the facilitator closes collection, nobody on your team can read what you wrote — the facilitator included. Then the sprint sees every thought at once, in random order, without names.

Encrypted on your team’s devices

New sprints are encrypted in the browser before anything reaches Muni’s servers, and the servers don’t hold the keys to read them. Muni still knows who wrote each thought and when, sprints from before encryption aren’t covered, and your wording can still give you away.

No password, no email

You sign in with a passkey — your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It’s the only way in: there’s no password or email to steal.

No ads, no trackers, no AI

The app has no analytics, ad trackers or session recording, and no thought is sent to an AI service.
