How Muni works

  1. 01 During the sprint

    Set it down while it’s fresh.

    A thought takes ten seconds to write on Tuesday and is gone by Friday. Muni keeps a quiet place open all sprint — on your phone, between tasks, in a room of your own if you pick one of eight characters. If the signal drops, it waits and sends when you’re back, and tells you plainly when a thought has reached the sprint.

  2. 02 Sealed

    Your team can’t read them yet.

    Not the facilitator, not your manager. Until collection closes, the only thoughts you can see are your own — and you can still change them.

  3. 03 Collection closes

    One batch. Random order. No names.

    When the facilitator closes collection, everyone’s thoughts appear at once, shuffled. Nothing on screen says who wrote a thought, or ties one person’s thoughts together.

  4. 04 Before the retro

    Thoughts become themes.

    If it helps, the facilitator ticks the thoughts that belong together and names them — a theme, with a question to open it. Every thought stays exactly as written.

Week 1Week 2

05 The retro

One topic. The actual words.

The shared screen puts what people wrote at the centre, not a summary of it — and when a check-in is shared, what everyone answered lands right there in the topic, without names.

  • Four steps, one question each

    Did last time’s experiments help? What matters most? Then the talk, topic by topic, and what to try. The steps open and close the vote themselves.

  • Votes on every phone

    Everyone picks what to talk about first, privately. Nobody sees a count until the room moves on.

  • Disagreement stays visible

    Opposing views sit side by side in their own words. Nothing is averaged into a paragraph, and nobody is called on.

06 Quiet ways in

Everyone answers. Nobody has to speak first.

When it helps, the facilitator asks how a topic showed up for people. One tap is a whole answer; a line is optional. Nothing is shown until the facilitator shares it — and never with a name.

  • One tap is enough. Writing and speaking stay optional, and nothing waits for everyone.
  • Private until shared. The facilitator sees how many answered, never who.
  • Add without the floor. An example, another view or a question, shared when the moment’s right.
  • A concern isn’t outvoted. When an idea is checked, a concern stays beside the rest.

Privacy

Your reflections are for your team.

Your reflections are for your team.

Muni uses your account information and contributions to provide and protect the service. We don’t sell your personal data, use your contributions for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

Sealed, then shared without names

Until the facilitator closes collection, nobody on your team can read what you wrote — the facilitator included. Then the sprint sees every thought at once, in random order, without names.

Encrypted on your team’s devices

New sprints are encrypted in the browser before anything reaches Muni’s servers, and the servers don’t hold the keys to read them. Muni still knows who wrote each thought and when, sprints from before encryption aren’t covered, and your wording can still give you away.

No password, no email

You sign in with a passkey — your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It’s the only way in: there’s no password or email to steal.

No ads, no trackers, no AI

The app has no analytics, ad trackers or session recording, and no thought is sent to an AI service.
