# Using Muni

A guide for teams running retrospectives in Muni, at https://act.munimuni.app. It opens in any
modern browser. Installing it is optional.

## Getting started

Muni signs you in with a **passkey**. There is no password and no email sign-in.

1. Choose **Create an account**, enter a name, then **Create with a passkey**.
2. Add a second passkey when Muni offers (**Add a second passkey**). If you lose every passkey, you lose
   the account.
3. To start a team, choose **New workspace** (a workspace is one team), then **Set up a sprint**:
   a name, the dates and the retro time.
4. Invite your team (see [Bringing people in](#bringing-people-in)) and open collection.

New accounts see a short prologue. Until your team's first retro is done, a small light (a
firefly) then sits on the next control to use and says what it is for. **Later** puts a step off.
**Hide the guide** hides it. To bring it back, open the account menu and choose **The first
evening**.

## Writing thoughts

Open Muni and your sprint has a place to write. Write first. A category (Proud of, Keep, Improve,
Stop or Try) is optional, and tapping it again clears it. **Context** adds the impact, what might
help, and when in the sprint it happened. Press **⌘/Ctrl + Enter** to save.

- Your thoughts stay hidden from your teammates, and from the facilitator, until the facilitator
  closes collection. You can edit or delete them until then.
- If more than one sprint is collecting for you, Muni asks where a thought goes. It never picks
  silently.
- Offline, **Add to sprint** keeps the thought on your device and sends it when the connection
  returns, when you open Muni, or when you press the status chip in the header (*Offline*, *N
  waiting* or *N to review*). Retries never create duplicates. A thought shows as **Submitted**
  only after the server has it.
- If collection closes before a waiting thought arrives, Muni does not send it. It stays on your
  device as *Needs attention*. Copy it, send it to another collecting sprint, or discard it.

### Keeping drafts on this device

**Account → This device → Keep drafts on this device** is off by default.

- **On:** drafts and waiting thoughts are kept in this browser for your account. You can close
  Muni and continue later, and open it offline. Use it on a personal device only: anyone who
  can use this browser profile can read them.
- **Off:** drafts and waiting thoughts last only as long as the tab. Muni warns you when closing it
  would lose something.

**Clear local data** and **Sign out** remove what this device keeps for your account. They warn you
first if something is unsent, and delete nothing on the server.

### What needs a connection

Signing in for the first time, accepting an invitation, creating workspaces or sprints, closing or
reopening collection, voting, editing a submitted thought, and everything in a live retro. If the
connection drops during a retro, Muni shows *Reconnecting to the retro…* and queues nothing.

## The sprint page

Every sprint has one page for everyone in it. Open it from **Sprints**, from a reminder email, or
with **Write**, which opens the sprint that is collecting for you.

The bar at the top shows the stage: *Not open yet*, *Collecting thoughts*, *Collection closed*,
*Retro in progress* or *Retro done*, with the retro's **planned** date. The date is a plan only.
Nothing changes until the facilitator makes the change.

### Facilitating

The bar shows the one next change and what it will do:

- **Open collection** lets everyone in the sprint add thoughts.
- **Close collection…** reveals every thought to everyone in the sprint and stops adding and
  editing. You do not have to wait for the planned date. It does not start the retro.
- **Reopen collection…** (under **More**) lets people write again until the retro starts. What was
  already revealed stays visible. Once the retro has started, collection cannot reopen.
- **Start the retro…** opens the stage when the team is together. Before that, **Group into
  themes** if you want topics to vote on. Thoughts left out of any theme become one more
  topic, *Everything else*. With no themes, everything is one topic, *Everything we wrote*.
- **Open the stage** returns you to a running retro. **More → Pause the retro…** stops it.
  **Start the retro again…** on the stage restarts from Look back, keeping notes and experiments.
- **More → Invite people** adds people to an unfinished sprint.
- **More → Archive sprint…** moves a finished sprint to earlier sprints.
- **More → Take over facilitating…** is for workspace owners when the facilitator cannot carry on, for
  example after losing every passkey. The owner joins the sprint if needed and runs it from then
  on. The old facilitator stays as a participant. In an encrypted sprint that is still collecting,
  the owner's device starts a new key for later thoughts. Thoughts already written stay sealed to
  the old facilitator's key, and the dialog says how many.

### The reveal

When collection closes, the page shows **Everyone's thoughts**: every thought, without names, in
a random order. Yours are among them. Reading them before the retro leaves the meeting for talking.

Anyone still writing when collection closes keeps their text on screen. Nothing is sent.

## Bringing people in

Workspace owners open **People → Invite**:

- **By email** sends a link that works once, for one address, and expires in 14 days.
- **With a link or QR** makes either a team QR, where people ask to join and an owner approves
  them, or a personal link for one person (24 hours or 7 days).

Owners also see invitations still waiting and can withdraw them. A sprint's facilitator can invite
people into that sprint while it is unfinished, by email or with the sprint's own code. Everyone
joins as a member.

## The retro

The facilitator opens the stage on a shared screen. Press **H** to hide its controls when
presenting. Everyone else chooses **Join the retro** on the sprint page, on their own phone or
laptop. The stage never takes votes or answers, not even the facilitator's. The facilitator uses
**Your own votes and answers** on the stage, or **Vote and answer** on the sprint page.

Opening the retro marks you present, and your face lights up on the stage. The facilitator can mark
someone present who has no device.

The retro has four steps, each one question:

1. **Look back:** did last time's experiments help? The facilitator records *Helped*, *Didn't
   help*, *Inconclusive* or *Not tried yet* for each. Thoughts marked Proud of or Keep show beside
   them. A team's first retro has no experiments to review. The sprint's opening question, if it
   has one, shows while people arrive.
2. **Choose:** what matters most? Everyone votes privately on their phone, at most once per
   theme. Nobody sees the result until the step ends, and the facilitator sees only how many
   have voted. Each person gets the sprint's **Votes per person** (3 by default), but never more
   than half the topics, rounded down. **Vote again** clears the votes and opens a new round. With
   fewer than two themes, this step is skipped.
3. **Talk:** one topic at a time, flagged topics first, then in vote order. Each topic shows its
   thoughts as written and a clock that is guidance, not a cut-off. The facilitator records **We'll
   remember** and, if there is one, **We could try**. The facilitator can also ask the room, always
   optionally:
   - **Ask how it showed up.** Phones ask *How did this show up for you?* with *I felt this*, *Not in
     my work* or *I'd need context*, and an optional line.
   - **Check it with the room.** Once there is a **We could try**, phones ask *Would trying this next
     sprint help?* with *Worth trying*, *I have a concern* or *Not sure*. A concern is shown
     beside the rest, never outvoted.
   - **Add to this discussion.** Anyone can add an example, another view or a question from their
     phone at any time.

   Answers can be changed until the facilitator chooses **Share answers**. They show as counts and
   lines, without names. Additions wait, without names, until the facilitator shares them.
4. **Agree:** what will we try? The ideas from the talk wait here. Aim for one to three experiments.
   Each has an owner, who says yes on their own phone. **End the retro** shows everyone that it is
   done.

**The facilitator's cue.** At the foot of the facilitator's own screen, a cue gives a line to say
and the one thing to do next. Follow it or not. **Hide lines to say** keeps only the next step.

**Moving around.** The facilitator moves on with **Next** or →, and back with ←. The arrow keys
do nothing while a dialog, menu or field is open. A topic's answers and clock are kept when you
return to it. **+2** adds two minutes to the clock. When two people facilitate, the one leading has
the controls and the other's stage follows until they choose **Take over**. If you join late,
reconnect or pick up your phone again, Muni puts you where the room is, with anything you wrote
still there.

## The recap

When the retro ends, the sprint page becomes its recap, the same for everyone in it:

- who came
- **What we'll try**: each experiment, its owner and its review date
- **What we talked about**, topic by topic
- **In the facilitator's words**, once the facilitator publishes it. The facilitator can start a draft
  with **Fill in from the record** and edit it before publishing.

Downloads at the foot of the page (**Summary .md**, **Summary .csv**, and **Raw notes .md** for the
facilitator) carry no authors, times or individual votes. They carry totals, and experiment owners
by name. Saved files are not encrypted.

Experiments come back first in the next sprint's **Look back**.

## Leaving

- **A workspace:** People → **⋯** → **Leave workspace…**, or **Delete and leave** if you are its only
  member. What you wrote stays with the team.
- **Your account:** Account → **Delete account…**, type *delete*, confirm with your passkey, then
  choose **Delete my account**. What nobody has seen yet goes with you: unrevealed thoughts,
  unshared additions, and votes and answers not yet counted. What your team has already seen stays,
  tied to no one.

If something the team relies on would be left without anyone, Muni says what to hand on first. That
applies to the last owner of a workspace, and to the facilitator of an unfinished sprint others are
in.

## Installing Muni

Choose **Install Muni** in the account menu. Where the browser offers an install prompt, it appears.
Elsewhere Muni shows the steps:

- **iPhone or iPad:** in Safari, Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Mac, Safari:** File → *Add to Dock*.
- **Chrome or Edge on desktop:** the install icon at the right of the address bar.
- **Firefox on Android:** menu → *Install*. Firefox on desktop cannot install web apps.

The installed app opens in its own window and updates itself. It never reloads while you type,
while something is sending, or during a live retro.

## Limits

- Muni is in English only.
- It is tested on Chrome and Firefox on desktop, Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. The
  automated suites run in Chromium.
- Finished sprints are deleted automatically (after 90 days by default; owners can set 7 to 3,650).
  There is no way to delete one sooner. Unfinished sprints are not deleted automatically.
- There is no download of everything you have written. Ask the contact on the Privacy page.
- If you lose every passkey, the account cannot be recovered, and nobody can reset it. An optional
  recovery key restores encrypted writing on a device you can still sign in on, not the account.
- In a sprint set up without encryption, the operator can technically read thoughts. In an encrypted
  sprint, they cannot.
- The encryption has not been independently audited. See [privacy-claims.md](privacy-claims.md).

Who can see what, and what the operator and service providers can access, is on **Account →
Privacy & data** (https://act.munimuni.app/privacy).
